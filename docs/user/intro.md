# Welcome to InterNovel

InterNovel is a revolutionary collaborative writing platform designed for authors, world-builders, and storytellers. It empowers you to co-create narratives, share intricate worlds, and retain absolute, undeniable ownership of your intellectual contributions.

## Core Concepts

### 1. Local-First Architecture
Your writing belongs to you. InterNovel is built on a "local-first" philosophy. This means your data lives on your device first and foremost. You can write offline, and when you connect, your work syncs seamlessly peer-to-peer with your collaborators using the Willow protocol. There is no central server holding your manuscript hostage.

### 2. True Ownership (Decentralized Identity)
In collaborative projects, attribution can get messy. InterNovel solves this using Decentralized Identifiers (`did:peer`). Every contribution you make is cryptographically signed by your unique, device-bound identity. This ensures that your work is immutably attributed to you, providing clear, undeniable proof of authorship.

### 3. The Novel Spine
Writing a novel is rarely a linear process. The "Novel Spine" allows you to develop ideas, scenes, and character arcs non-linearly while maintaining an underlying chronological structure. You can jump between chapters, leave placeholders, and weave multiple storylines together without losing track of the overarching narrative flow.
